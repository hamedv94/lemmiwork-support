Set up Music

  1. Open Lemmiwork and click Continue in Welcome and again on Music access.
  2. When macOS asks if Lemmiwork can control Music, click Allow.
  3. On Let Lemmiwork use Apple Music, click Continue and allow access to save streamed songs, or choose Not Now to finish setup.
  4. On Players, choose the Floating player, the Menu bar player or both. Click Done, then play a song in Music.

If you see Music access is off, click Open Automation Settings and turn on Music under Lemmiwork. The welcome window continues on its own.

  • macOS 13 or later: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation
  • macOS 12: System Preferences → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Automation

If the prompt closes without an answer, click Ask Again. If access is turned off later, choose Allow Lemmiwork to Control Music… from either player’s menu.

Set up your players

Open Settings… from either player’s ••• menu. Right-click also opens that menu, even if the button is hidden. Choose Show Tips to see labels for the controls.

In Floating Player and Menu Bar settings, you can turn each player on or off and choose which buttons appear. For the floating player, choose its orientation, size and time display under ••• → Layout. Turn on Keep on Top to keep it visible over full-screen apps. Drag its three-line handle to move it, or select ⌘ Command-drag under Move with and hold Command while dragging anywhere on the player.

Lemmiwork keeps running when you close Settings. If both players are off, open Lemmiwork from Applications or Spotlight to get back to Settings. The widget still works. To start Lemmiwork automatically, choose Open at login in General.

Play and see what’s playing

Use either player to play, pause or skip. On the floating player, click the progress line to jump within a song. Choose Shuffle or Repeat from either player’s menu.

Hover over the artwork to see the song details, or click it to open Music.

Favorite, save or share a song

  • Click the star to add or remove a Favorite. Click + to add the song to your library. A checkmark means it’s already there.
  • Hover over Add to Playlist in the player’s menu to choose a playlist under Recent or browse your folders. After you’ve saved a song to a playlist in Lemmiwork, use Last Playlist to save to that playlist again. You can show that button in either player’s settings.
  • Choose Share Song… to open the Mac’s share sheet, or Copy Link to copy the Apple Music URL. If Lemmiwork can’t find the URL, Share Song… shares the song details as text.

To save a streamed song, use Add to Library or Add to Playlist with an Apple Music subscription. Choose a playlist you can edit. With your permission, MusicKit uses your Music User Token and sends the save requests directly from your Mac to Apple’s API. The developer receives no data from these requests. Lemmiwork has no server, analytics or tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Start the trial, buy or restore

All of Lemmiwork is free for 7 days, then US $3.99 once (or your App Store’s local price). No subscription.

After Welcome, click Start Free Trial and confirm with Apple. If you close the sheet, click either player or the widget link to open it again.

After 7 days, Lemmiwork locks without charging you. Click Buy Lemmiwork on either player or the widget to open the purchase sheet.

In Settings… → About, you can also start the trial, check its end date, buy or choose Restore Purchase with the Apple Account you bought it with. Apple handles billing and refunds. Apple’s refund instructions

Set keyboard shortcuts

In Settings… → Shortcuts, click Record Shortcut beside Add to Favorites, Add to Library or Add to Last Playlist. Use a combination with Command or Control. Click × to clear a shortcut. If the combination conflicts with another app, choose a different one.

You can set up shortcuts at any time. They work from other apps while Lemmiwork is running with an active trial or purchase.

Add the widget

Click the date and time in the menu bar, choose Edit Widgets, then search for Lemmiwork and add the medium widget. These steps are also in Settings… → Widget. Apple’s guide to Mac widgets

On macOS Sonoma 14 or later, the widget can play, pause and skip while Lemmiwork is running with permission to control Music. When Lemmiwork is locked, click its widget link to open the trial or purchase sheet. Use ••• for Add to Playlist, Share Song… and Copy Link.

On macOS Monterey 12 and Ventura 13, click Track Options to open the song menu in Lemmiwork, including Favorite and Add to Library.
